## What the research changed

Italy is **not a cold market**. It is a warm one that nobody has ever answered.

`avventurosamente.it` — the biggest Italian general-outdoor community — already
carries a **Freemap entry in its Risorse directory**, created 11 Apr 2023 by user
*Skelter*, **5 088 views**, never updated since:

> https://www.avventurosamente.it/xf/risorse/freemap.495/
> Link: `https://www.freemap.sk/#map=9/43.188156/12.310181&layers=X`

And Freemap comes up unprompted in their map threads, favourably:

- *"Tra quelle che ho messo freemap.sk mi sembra la più simile a Mapy, ed è anche
  quella fatta meglio."* — Sustuso
- *"Sì, ha una grafica molto buona."* — AleZan
  (both in the **MOBAC / 4UMaps** thread, `/xf/threads/…-4umaps.62858/`)

Three conclusions drive everything below.

1. **Freemap is already known here, and liked.** So there is nothing to introduce.
   Anything that reads as "let me present my project" is redundant to the people who
   already use it and ad-shaped to everyone else. What they do *not* know is what
   changed since 2023 — that is the post with a right to exist.
2. **The strongest thread is a rescue, not an introduction.** `4UMaps` shut down and
   Italians are still looking for a replacement offline source. That is an audience
   with an unmet need, already discussing us.
3. **Every Italian pointer says `freemap.sk`.** New arrivals are better sent to
   `freemap.eu`, so every link written from here uses it. Existing users are a
   different population and should be left alone — see the storage note below.
   Whether the `.sk` branding costs anything is unverified; the forum evidence says
   they got on with it fine.

Everything below is written to **attract, never to sell**: no prices, no premium,
no feature gating mentioned. The map does the converting.

**The lead is the trails, not the terrain.** Freemap colours paths from
`osmc:symbol` (so CAI white-red renders as white-red), labels them from `ref` and
`name` — i.e. the CAI number is written on the map — and dashes local trails.
Avventurosamente has asked for exactly this more than once and never got a good
answer. Terrain is the second punch, not the first.

Each draft pairs that claim with its honest limit: where OSM lacks the tag, the
number is missing — and the reader can add it and see it minutes later. That
sentence costs nothing, pre-empts the one objection, and recruits mappers.

Affiliation is disclosed in every post. On Italian forums an undisclosed plug that
gets found out is fatal; a disclosed one that is actually useful is welcomed.

## A product-side lever worth more than any post

Every Italian mention points at `freemap.sk`, and the serving domain expands the
`{site}` placeholder — so an Italian following the Risorse entry lands on a page
calling itself **Freemap Slovakia**. That one word does more damage to foreign
adoption than a missing forum post: it says "Slovak map" to someone standing in
the Dolomites over 5 m terrain of their own country.

That entry alone has sent 5 088 views to the wrong door.

**But moving existing users is off the table.** Storage is origin-scoped, so
anyone sent from `.sk` to `.eu` lands on a blank origin: cached offline tiles,
localStorage prefs, custom layers and IndexedDB state all stay behind. A signed-in
account restores settings and saved maps from the server; downloaded offline tiles
it does not. A redirect would therefore cost real users real data for a branding
gain — don't.

Any hint would have to be limited to visitors with nothing to lose (no IndexedDB,
no localStorage, first visit), and even then it is optional. Cross-origin
migration via an iframe handshake is technically possible but not worth it for
tile caches this large.

**So the lever is inbound links, not existing users.** Every new Italian arriving
through a post written from here can be pointed at `.eu` from the start, with no
data to migrate. Every draft in this file already links only to `freemap.eu`, and
the Risorse-entry request to Skelter is exactly that: fixing where *future*
readers land, not relocating anyone who is already settled in.

Unverified, and worth settling in Matomo before acting on it at all: whether
Italians on `.sk` actually engage worse than on `.eu`. The forum evidence says
they liked it fine on `.sk`.
