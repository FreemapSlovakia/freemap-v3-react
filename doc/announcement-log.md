# Feature-announcement log

What has shipped, what has been posted about it, and where.

**Three channels, three audiences, managed separately — but all tracked here.**

- **Facebook** (`facebook.com/FreemapSlovakia`) — Slovak, in Slovak. The big audience:
  400–17,000 reach per post. Wants practical, local, usable-this-weekend.
- **Mastodon** (`@FreemapSlovakia@en.osm.town`) — everyone else, in English. Small
  following (~64) but boosts carry it into the international OSM network. Wants terrain,
  coverage, open data.
- **osm_sk** — the Slovak OSM forum, written to as a mailing list
  (`osm_sk@googlegroups.com`) and read as
  [groups.google.com/g/osm_sk](https://groups.google.com/g/osm_sk); 435 members. Slovak,
  plain prose, no visual. Few readers but the ones who map, so it takes the data and
  rendering news the Page would find dry, and its threads come back as bug reports and
  tagging questions.

A topic can go to more than one, but never as the same text: each gets its own language
and angle. Some belong to only one — Slovak mobile-app releases never reach Mastodon,
foreign terrain updates are wasted on the Page. The per-channel evidence is in _What
actually performs_ below.

Four lists:

- **Backlog** — post-worthy shipped features, newest first, with the commits that carry
  them and which channel they suit. A topic leaves the backlog when posted or deliberately
  skipped.
- **Posted — Mastodon**, **Posted — Facebook** and **Posted — osm_sk** — one row per post,
  per channel.

Market-intro posts ("Freemap.eu exists, here is what it does") belong in the Outreach log
in [`promotion-roadmap.md`](./promotion-roadmap.md), not here. This file is for
"here is what's new".

## How to maintain it

- Add a backlog entry when a user-visible feature lands, with its commit hashes. Group the
  follow-up fixes under the feature they belong to — one topic, not one commit.
- Size a topic to a post: a headline feature worth its own visual, or a bullet in another
  post's tail. Mark which.
- On posting, move the topic to the **Posted** table of the channel it went to, with the
  date and URL, and keep the commit hashes with it — that is what makes the log worth
  having. A topic posted to both gets a row in each; leave it in the backlog until it has
  gone to every channel its `Channel` value names.
- Map rendering ships from the `freemap-outdoor-map` repo and is announced too. Its commits
  go in the same column, prefixed `outdoor-map:` (`outdoor-map: 7f98c81`); unprefixed
  hashes are this repo's. Data-pipeline scripts are not a topic until the app credits the
  dataset.
- When catching up, check both repos: `git log --since=<last catch-up>` here and in
  `../freemap-outdoor-map`, keeping the commits whose hash prefix this file lacks. Rows group
  commits by topic and miss some, so an absent hash means "check the topic", not "add a row".
- Keep `—` where a date or link is unknown rather than guessing.
- Word every post and mail by the manual's [writing rules](./manual.md#writing): formal
  address, UI labels verbatim, and no map, tool or app acting like a person — "od
  priblíženia 16 sa na mape kreslí …", not "mapa kreslí …". The exception is its no-emoji
  rule: Facebook and Mastodon posts lead with emoji.
- Mastodon (`en.osm.town`) caps a post at 500 characters, so a post is one headline plus a
  short tail. Facebook has no cap but shows one visual, so the same shape applies. The
  mailing list has neither cap nor visual, so it can carry the detail the other two drop.
- The Posted rows can be re-read without auth:
  `curl -s 'https://en.osm.town/api/v1/accounts/116063901680527111/statuses?limit=25&exclude_replies=true'`
  — that is also where the ♥/↻ counts come from.

## Tooling

**Posting is manual on both channels.** The tooling below is for reading — filling in this
file's reach figures, and checking what has already gone out.

- **Mastodon** needs no auth at all: the curl above refreshes the Posted rows and their ♥/↻
  counts any time. No token, no server.
- **Facebook** is read from Meta Business Suite in the browser — see
  [`facebook-insights-prompt.md`](./facebook-insights-prompt.md), which is a
  ready-to-paste prompt for Claude in Chrome (or a checklist to follow by hand). Prefer its
  CSV export over reading figures off the screen. A row's permalink is not in the table:
  open the post's insights page and take the href behind the relative timestamp.
- **osm_sk** takes two sources. _What was announced_ comes from Gmail, since the
  announcements go out from `martin.zdila@freemap.sk`: search
  `to:osm_sk@googlegroups.com from:me after:2026/01/01` and keep the threads whose sender is
  that address — the rest are other people's posts to the list. _The archive URL_ comes from
  the forum itself, where a thread is `groups.google.com/g/osm_sk/c/<id>`.
  **Filter the forum by author, not by label.** The `Novinky_z_Freemap` label looks like the
  announcement stream and is not: five of 2026's went out unlabelled or under another label
  (_Multimodálne routovanie_ is filed under `WeeklyOSM`). `/g/osm_sk/search?q=from:martin.zdila@freemap.sk`
  finds them all, though it also returns threads he only replied in — which is why Gmail,
  not the forum, decides what belongs in the table.
  A thread carries a **view count** in its header when opened, the one audience figure this
  channel has; it is not in any list view, so collecting it costs one page per thread.
- **Images.** A map-only picture comes from the app's own _Map export to image_ (visible
  area, 192 DPI → 2400 px wide, no app chrome, the credits drawn in); a picture of the UI
  from DevTools' _Capture screenshot_, which keeps the screen's pixel ratio. Browser-tool
  screenshots are CSS-sized and too small for either channel. Facebook wants 4:5, Mastodon
  16:9.

**Do not retry the Facebook Graph API** (attempted 2026-08-13). Reading a Page's own posts
and insights needs `pages_read_engagement` and `read_insights`, and Meta no longer offers
either to an ordinary app: in the Login-for-Business permission picker only
`pages_show_list` and `business_management` appear, and the "+ Add" button leads to
_become a Tech Provider_ — irreversible, and gated behind business verification plus App
Review. Verified against a purpose-made app (Freemap MCP, unpublished, "Manage everything
on your Page" use case) with the Page's own admin; `pages_show_list` was the only Page
scope granted. Two further traps found on the way: the app-type gate means the existing
`Freemap` login app (Consumer type, App ID `171410630094006`, wired up in
`src/features/auth/model/processors/fbLoader.ts`) cannot do Pages API at all, and claiming
the Page into the Freemap Slovakia business portfolio — which the system-user route
requires — would have removed three existing people from the Page.

Meta's own [MCP servers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/mcp) do not change
this: the documented one (Meta Devtools MCP) manages apps, webhooks, compliance and doc
search, and the other is Ads. Neither reads a Page's posts or insights, and no MCP can grant
a permission the platform withholds.

## House style (@FreemapSlovakia on Mastodon)

Read off the account's own posts, so a new draft matches what is already there:

- English only, 1–4 images or one animation, one post per topic — no changelog dumps.
- An emoji-led headline that asks the user's question ("How hard will that climb really
  be? ⛰️"), then 2–5 short lines, each opening with an emoji when it is a list.
- A link to `https://www.freemap.eu` — deep-linked with a `#map=…&layers=…` hash where the
  feature can be shown directly, which is stronger than the bare homepage.
- 3–6 hashtags, closing: `#OpenStreetMap` almost always, plus `#hiking` / `#cycling` /
  `#GIS` / `#OpenData` and the country tag in both languages (`#Croatia #Hrvatska`).
- Credit the data source by name and licence when a post is about data.
- **Say the mechanism in plain words.** This audience rewards knowing _how_ a feature works,
  but naming the plumbing is not the same as explaining it — `127.0.0.1` read as jargon even
  here, where "they talk on the phone itself — no server, no signal" says the same thing and
  carries the privacy and offline consequences with it.
- Awkward news gets posted too, plainly (the Strava-heatmap removal, 2026-06-12 — the
  account's most-boosted post).

## House style (the Facebook Page)

- **Facebook renders no formatting.** `**bold**`, `_italic_` and every other markdown mark
  is published verbatim, so a draft written with them has to be flattened before it goes
  out. Structure comes from emoji and line breaks alone: an emoji opens each item, and a
  short heading line above the sentences does the work bold would. Sparing CAPS is the one
  native emphasis, used about once a year (2026-06-02).
- **Don't reach for Unicode "bold" letters** (the U+1D400 maths block) to get around that.
  It covers A–Z, a–z and digits only, so every Slovak diacritic drops back to normal weight
  mid-word — `𝐕𝐲𝐡ľ𝐚𝐝á𝐯𝐚č 𝐭𝐫á𝐬` — which reads as a rendering fault. It also makes the post
  unsearchable by its own words and unreadable to a screen reader.
- Slovak throughout, `www.freemap.sk` as the link (not `.eu` — that is Mastodon's).
- **Don't open with a question.** Mastodon's house style above asks for one; the Page is
  not Mastodon, and drafts kept arriving in that shape until it was called out. The year's
  biggest posts open by saying something or by addressing someone — "Vychodňare, takoj
  vnimajce!" (17,137), "app 1.3 je vonku" (5,388), "Práve sme pridali podrobné modely
  terénu" (4,880) — while the question openers cluster at 500–1,000 (the cloud-inversion
  post, 9,263, is the one big exception). Lead with the concrete result instead: a figure,
  a legend row, the thing the reader gets.
- **Name a setting the way the app names it in Slovak.** Look the string up in
  `src/translations/sk.template.tsx` rather than translating it afresh, so a reader can find
  the thing by the words the post used — Predvoľby mapy, Krok priblíženia, Voľne, Objekty
  (POI), Vyhľadávač trás.
- Attaching a video makes Facebook publish the post as a reel; there is no choosing. See
  _What actually performs_ for when that is worth it.

## What actually performs — and the two audiences differ

From the Mastodon API plus a Business Suite post-level export (Jan–Aug 2026, 47 FB posts).
**The two channels reward opposite things, so the same post does not belong on both.**

- **Scale.** Facebook reach runs 400–17,000 per post against a Mastodon account of ~64
  followers. Facebook is where the volume is; Mastodon's value is boosts carrying a post
  into the international OSM network, not raw numbers.
- **Foreign-country terrain updates are Mastodon's best and Facebook's worst.** Poland's
  shading: 9♥/6↻ on Mastodon, 456 FB reach. Italy: 7♥/6↻, and 403 FB reach — the lowest of
  the year. The Slovak Page audience does not care about Polish contours; the international
  OSM crowd does.
  **But the subject is what fails, not the country.** England (2026-08-20) did **1,380 FB
  reach** — three times Poland or Italy — because the post was about what the new model
  _shows_: Iron-Age hillforts, Hadrian's Wall, roddons in the Fens. Terrain as archaeology
  travels on the Page; terrain as a resolution figure does not. Luxembourg (2026-08-28)
  confirms it from the other side: led on the model rather than on what it reveals, it did
  **538 reach and 0 link clicks** against 6♥/3↻ on Mastodon. Belgium (2026-09-02) is worse
  still — **263 reach**, the year's floor — and it _did_ lead on what the LiDAR shows: Ypres
  trench lines, the Butte du Lion. So England now reads as the exception rather than the
  rule: on the Page a foreign country's terrain does not travel, hook or no hook, while the
  same post is Mastodon's steady 6–10♥.
- **Slovak-relevant, practical, "go and use this today" posts are Facebook's best.** The
  cloud-inversion shading post — a mediocre 3♥/1↻ on Mastodon — did **9,263 reach, 127
  reactions and 266 link clicks** on Facebook. National terrain models: 4,880 reach.
- **The single biggest post of the year never went to Mastodon at all**: 2026-08-07,
  17,137 reach, 110 reactions, **426 link clicks** — the ÚGKK orthophoto update for eastern
  Slovakia, headlined in East-Slovak dialect ("Vychodňare, takoj vnimajce!"). Twice the next
  best. The lesson is the combination: a data update for one Slovak region, addressed to
  that region in its own voice. Repeatable, and nothing like it has been tried since.
- **Link clicks are the closest thing to a conversion signal** and they track practicality,
  not novelty: inversion 266, app launch 65, terrain models 23, most feature posts 0–10.
- **Video on Facebook is a lottery, not an upgrade.** Median reach by format: photos
  **1,193** (n=37), videos/reels **581** (n=7) — yet the single best post of the year is a
  video (17,137). High variance, and a _worse_ typical outcome than a photo. Don't assume an
  animation will travel just because it moves; the reliable floor is a photo post.
  **But "post or reel" is not a choice we make** — attaching a video to an ordinary Page
  post makes Facebook publish it as a reel. So this finding is a reason to ask whether a
  still can carry the feature, not a reason to prefer one upload path over another. Where
  the feature _is_ movement — a radar loop, a before/after sweep — a still cannot show it
  and video is the honest medium; take the variance.
- On Mastodon specifically, awkward news travels: the Strava-heatmap removal is the
  most-boosted post ever (10♥/10↻). Mastodon has no comparable format effect.
- **The mailing list has no metrics at all** — the only signal is whether a thread draws
  replies, and the ones that do are about rendering and tagging (the outdoor-map icon
  round drew a tag correction the same hour). It also runs behind: it last heard from us
  on 2026-08-16, so twelve Facebook posts have gone out since it was last told anything.

**So pick the lead per channel.** A terrain/coverage post leads on Mastodon; Facebook wants
the practical Slovak-usable feature, framed as a thing to do this weekend; the list wants
what changed in the data or the rendering, said plainly.

**The colorize round went out on both visual channels on 2026-08-31** — Facebook in the
morning, Mastodon the same day with the OSM-tags angle. What is left of it above is for
📧 list only: the steepness scale and the elevation profile are now digest lines, the

hiking/cycling post).

Four things learned writing the pair, worth not rediscovering:

- **A route's colorize mode could not be put in a URL**, so no link could show a coloured
  route. That is why `route-colorize-by=` and the legend flag now exist, and why the legends
  no longer live inside their toolbars — all added the same day, for these two posts.
- **A shared link is inert for a reader without premium.** The gate applies to drawing the
  colours, not only to choosing the mode, so a signed-out reader gets a plain line and no
  legend. On both channels the screenshot, not the link, does the work.
- **The pushing-bike dashes are hidden whenever a colorize mode is on**, so a post about
  colouring cannot show them in the same picture. The marked-stretch highlight dims the line
  the same way, for the same layering reason.
- **Pick the demo route in the app, not from a map in your head.** Two were drafted and
  thrown away: a rail-trail (90 % one surface, so the colours said nothing) and a Garda loop
  that sent a bicycle over a 1,607 m summit on a hiking path. It wants both a spread of
  surfaces and a ride someone would plausibly do.
