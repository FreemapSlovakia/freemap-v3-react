Many actions are faster from the keyboard. The keys work anywhere on the page, except while you are typing into a field or a dialog is open.

## The map

- the arrow keys move the map, with <kbd>Shift</kbd> in bigger steps
- <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> zoom in and out
- <kbd>.</kbd> opens and closes :FaLayerGroup: **Map layers**
- <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> hides and shows what is drawn on the map (:MdOpacity: **Data layer opacity**)
- <kbd>Del</kbd> deletes the selected item or point
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes a dialog, ends picking a point, deselects the selected item, or closes the route finder, drawing or map details – one step per press
- a map with a shortcut switches on and off with it; the shortcut is shown next to the map in the map menus and can be set in **Map manager**

## Two-key shortcuts

Most shortcuts are two keys pressed one after another within two seconds – for example <kbd>g</kbd>, then <kbd>r</kbd> opens the route finder. The first key stands for the kind of thing that follows:

- <kbd>g</kbd> – tools, e.g. <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>r</kbd> :FaRoute: route finder, <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>l</kbd> :MdPolyline: line drawing, <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>c</kbd> :FaEraser: clear the map
- <kbd>e</kbd> – export and sharing, e.g. <kbd>e</kbd> <kbd>p</kbd> :FaPrint: export the map as an image or document, <kbd>e</kbd> <kbd>g</kbd> :FaFileExport: export map data
- <kbd>m</kbd> – maps, e.g. <kbd>m</kbd> <kbd>m</kbd> :FaLayerGroup: map manager, <kbd>m</kbd> <kbd>l</kbd> :FaList: map legend
- <kbd>p</kbd> – photos, e.g. <kbd>p</kbd> <kbd>l</kbd> :FaUpload: browse photos from the newest, <kbd>p</kbd> <kbd>u</kbd> :FaUpload: upload photos
- <kbd>j</kbd> – open the place in another map, e.g. <kbd>j</kbd> <kbd>o</kbd> :SiOpenstreetmap: OpenStreetMap, <kbd>j</kbd> <kbd>c</kbd> :FaClipboard: copy the link

You don't need to remember them: menus, toolbars and the search box show each shortcut next to the item it opens.

## Photos

While viewing a photo, the left and right arrow keys move to the previous and next one, <kbd>s</kbd> shows the photo on the map, <kbd>m</kbd> opens it for editing, <kbd>f</kbd> switches to full screen and <kbd>Del</kbd> deletes a photo of yours.

## Undo

The browser's **Back** and **Forward** buttons step through your changes on the map, so they work as undo and redo; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> do the same.
