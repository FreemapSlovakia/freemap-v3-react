## Freemap

Freemap Slovakia is a project that brings together the Slovak OpenStreetMap community and operates this portal. It is represented by the civic association OZ Freemap Slovakia; its address and the people to contact are under :FaRegAddressCard: [Contacts](#show=about) in :FaBook: **Info & help**.

The main goal of the association is to support mapping and the creation of freely licensed maps, especially through:

- **promoting the international OpenStreetMap.org project** and supporting mapping, especially in Slovakia,
- **developing and operating the map portal www.freemap.sk**, a showcase of what open mapping achieves and a place for the community,
- **raising awareness of open maps** through seminars, conferences, printed materials and the media,
- **creating maps in various formats for different devices**, which brings new users and new mappers,
- **obtaining permissions and importing data from various sources**, adding to the information in open maps,
- **organizing training sessions, courses and workshops** for members, the public and organizations,
- **supporting education in cartography**, including meetings with experts from different fields,
- **supporting hardware and software development** for mapping and map tools,
- **developing mapping services** for municipalities, individuals and organizations.

Want to become a member? Visit [oz.freemap.sk](https://oz.freemap.sk/).

## OpenStreetMap

Almost all maps in Freemap are made from the data of [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org) – a free map database of the whole world, built and maintained by volunteers. Its goal is a detailed map that anyone may use, for anything from hiking and navigation to research, planning and education.

A mistake in these maps is therefore fixed in OpenStreetMap, not in Freemap.

## Contributing to OpenStreetMap

Anyone can contribute – by adding a road, a building, a cycling route or a point of interest in their area.

- [Start exploring in the iD web editor](https://www.openstreetmap.org/edit?editor=id) and join the mapping community.
- To edit the place you are looking at, right-click the map (or press and hold it on a phone) and choose :FaExternalLinkAlt: **Open in…** → **iD**.
- If you don't want to edit the map yourself, [report a map error](https://www.openstreetmap.org/fixthemap) and a mapper will look at it.

## Using the maps elsewhere

Many of the maps may be used in your own work, some even commercially, as long as you credit their sources (_attribution_), as their licences (CC, ODbL) require. Which sources to credit depends on what the map shows:

- :FaInfoCircle: at the bottom right of the map → :FaRegCopyright: **Copyright** lists the sources of the maps and data shown right now, with links.
- :FaPrint: [Map export to image/document](#show=map-to-document-export) lets you draw the credits into the image itself (**Attribution**), and once the map is ready it shows the sources to credit when you publish or share it, with **Copy attribution**.
