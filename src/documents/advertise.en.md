# Advertise with Us

Freemap is an outdoor map application used every day for planning trips, navigation and mapping. Advertising in it is one of the ways we keep the servers running and the map free for everyone — and it puts your message in front of an audience that is hard to reach elsewhere.

## Who you reach

Our visitors are hikers, cyclists, runners, skiers, geocachers, mushroom pickers, dog owners, rescuers and OpenStreetMap mappers. They come mostly from Slovakia, followed by Czechia, Poland, Hungary, Italy, Austria and other European countries.

It is an audience with a clear intent: people are here to plan or to find their way outdoors. That makes the slot a natural fit for outdoor gear and clothing, bike and ski shops and services, accommodation, tourist regions and attractions, events and races, and anything else tied to spending time outside.

The map runs in the browser on desktops, tablets and phones alike, and a large share of visits are long, working sessions rather than a quick glance — an ad here has time to be noticed.

## Where the ad appears

The ad is part of the map interface itself: it comes last in the strip of toolbars laid over the map, wrapping onto its own line below them. No pop-up, no overlay covering the map, nothing that has to be dismissed to keep using the application.

Several ads take turns in that one slot, each shown for half a minute at a time, with the share of appearances agreed in advance. Ads can be limited to visitors from a given country.

## Formats

The slot takes a **banner image**, or a short **text ad** — a headline with a sentence or two — or a combination of both. Everything is a link to your site.

We prepare the ad with you: send us a banner and the target link, or just tell us what you want to say and we will put the text together.

## How we report

We measure how many visits your ad was shown in and how many of them clicked it, and we pass those numbers on to you. Measurement runs on our own self-hosted analytics — no third-party ad networks, no trackers sold on, no personal data leaving our servers.

## What we do not accept

Nothing that would clash with what the map is for: no automatically playing sound or video, no animation stealing attention from the map, no misleading offers, and no third-party ad scripts — we serve the ad ourselves.

## Two things worth knowing

Ads are not shown to holders of the Freemap Premium subscription, nor in maps embedded in other websites. The reported figures reflect this.
